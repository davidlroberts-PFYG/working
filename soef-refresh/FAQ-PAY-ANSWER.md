## The answer to paste

**How do you get paid, and are you a fiduciary?**

Advisory services are offered by Plan For Your Goals, LLC, a State of Florida registered investment adviser. As an investment adviser, the firm has a fiduciary duty to put your interests first in the advice it gives.

The firm can be paid in three ways. You should know all three.

**Planning fees.** Planning clients pay fees set out in a signed agreement. The firm sends you an invoice. It does not take fees out of your accounts.

**A share of an outside manager's fee.** If you choose to have investments managed by an outside manager the firm recommends, that manager deducts its fee from your account and pays a share of it to the firm. That gives the firm a financial reason to recommend those managers, which is a conflict of interest.

**Insurance commissions.** David Roberts is also an independent licensed insurance agent and may receive commissions on insurance products; that conflict is disclosed in our Form ADV. When he recommends an insurance or annuity product, he acts as an insurance agent, not as a fiduciary investment adviser. You are never required to buy a product through him.

The firm's Form ADV Part 2A describes its services, fees and conflicts in full. You can download it here: [link: planforyourgoals.link/ADV]. Read it before you engage the firm, and ask about anything in it.

Any planning "commitments" we make refer to the planning process and service delivery, not to investment performance, tax results, or any specific financial outcome.
