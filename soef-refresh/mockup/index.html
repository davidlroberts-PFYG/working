Three questions sit under everything they look at.

  • What would a buyer pay for?

    Profit that holds up after someone is paid to do your job. Customers and contracts that stay when you step back.

  • Could it run without you?

    Who holds the customer relationships. What is written down. What happens to your certifications if your role changes.

  • Is your family secure without the sale?

    How much of your household’s income and retirement rests on the company, and what you would have if you couldn’t work.

About ten owners, eight weeks each, by invitation.
