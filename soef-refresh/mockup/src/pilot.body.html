What it is, and what it is not

It is

  • Planning advice about your business and your household, based on what you tell the firm.
  • An estimated value range, built from your profit and a range of multiples, with the basis and date shown beside each figure.
  • A first area to work on, and questions to take to your CPA and attorney.
  • Yours to keep, and to take to any other professional.

It is not

  • An appraisal or a valuation, or a tax or legal opinion.
  • For setting a price or terms for a sale, a buy-sell agreement or a loan, or for a tax or legal decision. Those call for a credentialed appraiser, your CPA and your attorney.
  • A recommendation to buy or sell any security or insurance product.
  • A prediction or promise of any sale price, value, multiple, profit, tax result, certification outcome, contract award or timing.

Questions owners ask first

What does it cost?

There is no fee. You will not be billed, and taking part does not require you to buy anything, now or later.

How much time does it take?

The online steps take about [your measured time], and you can stop and come back. The walk-through of your Value Briefing is about 45 minutes, with a shorter follow-up and a short feedback interview. The pilot runs eight weeks in all.

What information will you ask for?

Your own account of the business, your household and your plans, plus figures such as revenue, profit and owner pay from your last full year. You will not be asked for Social Security numbers, account or card numbers, tax IDs, passwords, the names of your employees or customers, or anything about a medical condition.

What happens when the eight weeks end?

The pilot agreement ends. Your Value Briefing is yours to keep and to take to any other professional. Your access to the app may end. David may describe the firm’s paid services, and you are free to decline.

Do I have to become a client afterward?

No. Taking part does not require you to buy anything or to continue with the firm. During the pilot itself you are a client of Plan For Your Goals, LLC under the pilot agreement, which is why you receive the firm’s Form ADV brochure before you sign.

Is this a valuation of my business?

No. The Value Briefing gives an estimated range for planning, built from your profit and a range of multiples. It is not an appraisal or a valuation, and it should not be used to set a price or terms for a sale, a buy-sell agreement or a loan, or for a tax or legal decision. Those call for a credentialed appraiser, your CPA and your attorney.
