**Added, clearly marked in the text:**

- **One proposed paragraph, for counsel.** It says the Terms cover the websites only, that advice is given only under a written agreement, and that nothing in the Terms waives rights under the securities laws. The last sentence is taken from your Pilot Planning Agreement, §14. Reason: the liability disclaimer and the indemnification clause speak of "services" and "the provision of or failure to provide services." Read broadly, that could look like an adviser limiting its liability to clients, which regulators treat as a problem when it could lead a client to believe they have given up rights they cannot give up. That is my understanding of the general principle, not a checked citation. Ask counsel or Comply.
- **Your WARRANTIES block,** word for word from your disclosure file, as its own section at the end. Your playbook calls for it on website terms, and it was not in the document you sent.

**Left exactly as written:** every other clause, including electronic communications, children, links, intellectual property, international users, indemnification, the liability disclaimer, termination, governing law and changes to terms.

## For counsel or Comply to look at

1. The proposed scope paragraph above, and whether the liability and indemnification clauses need narrowing for an investment adviser.
2. **Electronic communications.** The clause says using the Site is consent to receive all notices and disclosures electronically. Your Pilot Planning Agreement has its own e-delivery consent (§11) with a paper-copy right. The Terms should not be relied on for delivering the brochure or privacy notice.
3. **Sharing with third parties.** The links section says the firm may share a user's information with any third party it has a contract with. Check that against your Privacy Policy and privacy notice.
4. **Venue.** These Terms say "courts in Florida." The Pilot Planning Agreement says Santa Rosa County. They need not match, but decide on purpose.
5. **A "last updated" date,** which the document does not have.
